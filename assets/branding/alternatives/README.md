## Минимализм

[minimal.png](minimal.png)

Промпт:

```text
Use case: logo-brand. Create ONE new square Telegram avatar for a table-tennis expense-splitting bot with an Attack on Titan Survey Corps theme. Concept: elegant bold minimalist flat emblem. A single forest-green ping-pong paddle seen nearly front-on, tilted a little, with a beautifully clean simplified white Wings of Freedom insignia integrated into its rubber face; one small white ping-pong ball and one small warm gold coin subtly overlapping the lower edge. Off-white warm background, deep green and charcoal outlines, flat vector-like shapes, sophisticated sports-club identity, extremely restrained geometry. No gradients, no 3D, no glossy plastic, no shadow effects, no cloak, no characters, no lettering, no currency signs, no mockup, no watermark. Design a compact balanced emblem with large clear shapes legible at 48 pixels. All elements fit inside a central circular safe area with at least 15 percent edge padding. Full opaque square background. Deliver a single finished icon.
```

## Титан-талисман

[titan.png](titan.png)

Промпт:

```text
Use case: logo-brand. Create ONE new square Telegram bot avatar: a charming chibi mascot inspired by the Colossal Titan from Attack on Titan, enthusiastically holding a table-tennis paddle in one hand and a single gold coin in the other. A large rounded head and small upper body, friendly determined face, simplified stylized coral-red and ivory facial bands recognizable as the Colossal Titan, toy-like and entirely non-gory, no exposed anatomy, no frightening mouth. Crisp anime sticker illustration with confident dark outlines, a few bold cel-shaded shapes, warm cream and coral against a muted deep navy background. The ping-pong paddle is visibly a paddle with a solid circular face and short handle, not a tennis racket. Playful, readable and memorable, not realistic. No cloak, no full scene, no background props, no lettering, no watermark. Center the compact mascot in the inner 70 percent of the square so everything survives a circular Telegram crop. One polished finished avatar, not a sheet or mockup.
```

## Пиксельная иконка

[pixel.png](pixel.png)

Промпт:

```text
Use case: logo-brand. Create ONE new square Telegram avatar for a friendly table-tennis cost-splitting bot, with a clear Attack on Titan Wings of Freedom motif, in authentic pixel-art video-game item-icon style. A large diagonal navy and ivory ping-pong paddle, its face carrying a simplified white and dark blue two-wing Survey Corps emblem. One small white ping-pong ball and a bright gold coin placed alongside. Very crisp deliberately stepped pixel edges, coherent coarse pixel grid, limited 8-color palette, bold readable silhouettes, no anti-aliased edges, no painterly details, no fine noisy texture. Muted teal opaque background with generous empty space. Compact composition entirely within a central circular safe area. No cloak, no lettering, no numbers, no UI frame, no watermark, no scene. The result should feel like an excellent retro sports-game inventory icon. One image, not multiple panels.
```
